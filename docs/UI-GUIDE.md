# What every screen does

Sign in with your email and password. The left menu (a drawer behind the ☰ button on phones) has:
Today · Orders · Bills · Products · Customers · Craftsmen · Rates · Settings.
At the bottom of the menu: **English | العربية** switch, your name, and Log out.

The dark bar on top always shows today's **Hallmark gold, Tajabi gold and Silver** rate per tola.
If today's rates are not set it warns you and links to the Rates screen.

## Language (English / العربية)
Press **العربية** to switch the whole app to Arabic, right-to-left (menu on the right, mirrored arrows and forms).
The choice is remembered in the browser. Numbers stay 0-9, money shows as "… روبية".

## Today
* Greeting and date, buttons **New order** and **New bill**.
* Four cards: **Late orders**, **Ready for pickup** (finished, not delivered), **With craftsmen**, **Unpaid bills** (and how much to collect).
* **Needs attention**: open orders that are late or due within 7 days. Click one to open it.
* **Recent bills**, **Sales this month** (today / this month / unpaid), **Most sold items this month**, and a comparison with last month.

## Orders (things customers asked you to make)
* List with search (customer, phone, order number) and filters: status (New / In progress / Ready / Delivered), metal, due date (today, 7 days, late).
* **New order**: pick or type the customer (an existing one is found by name or phone, otherwise a new one is saved), metal, rate, promised date, advance paid, the customer's own metal, notes, and the items.
  Type an item name that exists in **Products** and its weight, stone and size fill in by themselves.
* **Order page**: progress of every item, design photo upload per item, **Give work** (hand an item to a craftsman), **Make bill**, **Mark delivered** (when no bill is wanted), Edit, Delete (only while new and without a bill).

## Bills (invoices)
* List with search and filters: paid / unpaid / draft, metal, date range. Shows balance.
* **New bill** (or **Make bill** from an order, which brings the items across): customer, metal and rate, items with weight, loss, making charge, stone price and size.
  The server works out each line = (weight + loss) × rate ÷ 100 + making + stone, then total, old-metal credit, discount, advance, paid now and balance. The summary box updates live.
  "Save as draft" keeps it without delivering the order.
* **Bill page**: printable invoice with QR code, signatures and balance due. **Record payment** for later payments (cannot exceed the balance), Print, Edit, Delete (the order goes back to "Ready").

## Products (new)
* A catalog of what you make: ring, chain, bangle… as cards with photo, metal, usual weight (lal), usual making charge, stone, size and a note.
* Each card shows how many times it was ordered and sold.
* **Add product** / edit (pencil) / delete (bin). Add or change a photo, or hide a product (it stops appearing in suggestions but stays in the list under "Hidden").
* Search by name, stone or note; filter Gold / Silver and Shown / Hidden.
* Deleting a product never changes old orders or bills, because they keep their own copy of each line.

## Customers
* List with orders, bills and how much each owes you. Search by name, phone or address.
* **Add customer**. A customer page shows contact details, orders, bills, the unpaid total, quick **New order / New bill**, edit, and delete (only when there is no history).

## Craftsmen
* Who has your metal now and what they owe back. Tabs for work **with craftsmen** and **finished**, with search.
* **Give work**: choose the craftsman, an order and its item, metal given, KDM weight (added on top, e.g. solder), date given and date due. An item can only be with one craftsman.
* **Receive**: enter the weight brought back; the loss is worked out and can be corrected. The item then counts as finished.
* **Add craftsman**, edit, set a resign date (resigned staff cannot get new work). A craftsman page lists the full work history and total loss.

## Rates
* Set **today's** Hallmark, Tajabi and Silver rates (saving again replaces today's).
* Line chart of rate history (this month / this year) and a table of past rates.
* New orders and bills start from these rates; you can still type any other rate.

## Settings
* Your profile (name, phone, PAN number, email) which prints on bills, and **Change password**.
* Forgot password on the sign-in page sends a reset link by email.
